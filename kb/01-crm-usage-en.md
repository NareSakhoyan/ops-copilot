# Synthetic CRM usage rules
This document describes the demo CRM, not a real organization's policy.
Keep company, contact, lead, and deal records linked to the correct customer.
Review existing records before creating a duplicate. Emails and phone numbers are
private contact fields and must not be included in analytics answers.

## Tenant boundaries
Each organization can access only its own records. A tenant administrator works
within one tenant. An administrator role never authorizes another tenant's data.
