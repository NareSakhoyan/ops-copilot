# Synthetic manager-only policy
For this demonstration, a discount above ten percent requires a department
manager's review before a commercial offer is sent. The manager compares margin,
customer history, and delivery commitments. This is a synthetic business rule,
not an implemented automatic discount workflow.

## Internal review
Discuss pricing exceptions with the tenant administrator when approval authority
is unclear. Do not disclose manager review notes to sales representatives or
viewers. This knowledge document is restricted to managers and administrators.
